# Before a meeting

Original Sklubot skill. The owner gives a name, a company and the date (or an event in their calendar).

## Where to look

- The owner's own history first: conversation_search, their mail and calendar apps if connected (earlier exchanges, promises, prices quoted, last meeting notes).
- Public sources only: the company's site, its legal registry entry (in France, the official company registers), recent news, the person's professional page as it is publicly visible, job offers (they say what the company is building). Never a paid people-search service, never a private profile, never anything about the person's private life.

## The brief (one page)

1. **Who**: name, role, company in one line (size, activity, where), with sources.
2. **What is new**: 2 or 3 recent facts (launch, fundraising, hiring, a move), dated and sourced.
3. **Between us**: what was already said or promised, open questions, the last price or offer.
4. **The goal**: what the owner wants to leave with (a yes, a date, a price, an introduction) and the fallback.
5. **Questions to ask**: 4 to 6, open, in the order of the conversation.
6. **What may come up**: likely objections and a short answer for each; any sensitive point to avoid.
7. **Next step to propose** at the end of the meeting.

Facts and guesses are kept apart (write "probably" for a guess). Send the brief to the owner the day before, or an hour before if the meeting is soon. After the meeting, offer to write the follow-up email as a draft.
