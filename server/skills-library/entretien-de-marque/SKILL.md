# Brand interview

The owner knows their brand but has never written it down. You make it explicit, so every bot (and every designer or printer) works from the same page.

## Where it lives

- Notes per session: brand/<module>.md (file_save), with two parts: **Words** (their exact quotes and examples) and **Summary** (your reading, two or three possible phrasings, open questions).
- The final sheet: **charte.md** in the team folder (~/team/charte.md on your computer, so the other bots can read it); if there is no team folder, in your library, and tell the owner.

At the start of each session, read what exists, say in two sentences where you are, and ask "continue here or change topic?".

## How to interview

1. **One question at a time**, never a list (ask_user only when ready answers help; otherwise a plain question).
2. After each answer: a short paraphrase, then **one** deeper question, or close the topic.
3. **"Why does it matter to you?"** after each "what", two to four times, until a real value appears.
4. **Vague answer** ("quality", "passion", "close to customers")? Ask for one concrete example, a customer story or a number.
5. **Once per topic**, to unblock: "If the shop were a person, how would they walk into a room?", "If you closed in five years, what would customers miss?", "Name a competitor you respect but would never want to become, and why."
6. When two questions in a row bring nothing new, summarise and close the topic.

## The six topics (in this order, unless the owner wants to jump)

1. **Why**: why the business exists beyond making money; what made the owner start.
2. **Positioning**: what customers would use otherwise; what is truly different; for whom it is the obvious choice.
3. **Customers**: the best customer described as one person; what they want, fear, say.
4. **Personality**: five adjectives, and for each its limit ("warm, but never familiar").
5. **Voice and tone**: formal or familiar ("vous" or "tu" in French), words they use, words they never use, humour or not, how they answer a complaint.
6. **Story**: the true story in five sentences: before, the trigger, what they did, what changed, where they go.

## Voice profile from real texts

Ask for **5 to 10 real texts** the owner wrote or likes (emails that worked, posts, the website, a flyer). Recent ones first. Note: sentence length and rhythm; plain or detailed; how often numbers and proof appear; questions or not; punctuation habits; what they **never** do. Write it as a short block anyone can reuse. Never take another company's texts as the model.

## The brand sheet (charte.md)

One page, in this order: **In one sentence** (what, for whom, why different) · **Why** · **Customers** · **Personality** (adjectives and limits) · **Voice** (the profile, formal or familiar, words to use and avoid, three example sentences) · **Story** (five sentences) · **Look** (colours, fonts, logo: from "Brand sheet (design)" or what the owner gave) · **Date and who validated it**.

Show it, change what the owner corrects, and only call it final after their clear yes. Then add one memory sentence ("Brand sheet: ~/team/charte.md, validated on …").
