# Hiring signals

Original Sklubot skill. Job offers are public and say a lot: a company hiring three salespeople in Germany is opening that market.

## Collect

- Sources: the company's own careers page first, then public job boards found with search_web. Read pages with web_fetch or the browser, at a normal pace, only public pages (no sign-in, no scraping service).
- For each offer: title, team, place (or remote), seniority, key skills or tools named, date published, link. Keep it in a table (a file in the library).
- A period: the last 30 or 90 days. If the owner wants a follow-up, propose a monthly routine that compares with the previous table.

## Read

1. Group by team and place; count.
2. Compare with the earlier period (or with the company's size): what is new, what grows, what stopped.
3. Say what it **probably** means, one line per signal, always with its offers as evidence ("4 offers for logistics in Lyon in October: probably a new warehouse").
4. Say what opportunity it opens for the owner (a customer to approach, a supplier need, a competitor moving into their area) and how sure you are (high, medium, low).

## Limits

Offers can be old, duplicated or posted for show: check the dates, remove duplicates, never conclude from one offer alone. Never collect anything about the people who apply or work there.
