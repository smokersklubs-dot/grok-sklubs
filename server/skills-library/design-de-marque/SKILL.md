# Brand design

An original Sklubot skill.

## Finding the brand (in this order)

1. **The shared profile** (the "About the user" section of your context): company, preferences.
2. **Your memories**: memory_search for "brand", "colours", "logo", "style guide", "font", the company's name.
3. **Files**: the style guide, the logo or an earlier document the user gave you (your library, ~/team on your computer, or a folder they share through Sklubot Relais — only if they asked you to).
4. **Their website**: if they have an official site, open it (browser_open, page_read) to note the colours and fonts it really uses.
5. **Otherwise, ask** one short question (ask_user): "Do you have colours, a font or a logo to follow? If not, I will suggest a style."

## Applying it

- Use the exact values (hex codes, font names); do not "improve" them. When a brand colour lacks contrast for text (under 4.5:1), keep it for fills and accents and use a darker or lighter shade for text: say so.
- Logo: only the file the user gave, never redrawn or taken from another site. No logo given: the company's name in text.
- The tone of the texts follows the brand's (formal or familiar, sober or warm).
- Keep what you learned: memory_save in one sentence ("Brand X: blue #1D4E89, font Source Sans 3, sober tone"), or profile_update if the user wants the whole team to know.

## Never

- Use another company's brand, logo or colours (a client's or a well-known brand's) unless the user has the right to and asks for it.
- Invent a style guide and present it as the user's.
