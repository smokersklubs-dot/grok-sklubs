# SEO content

## Rules

- Never invent a search volume, a ranking, a statistic or a quote. Mark every assumption as an assumption and list the claims to verify before publishing.
- The owner's real experience is the edge: photos, cases, figures, customer questions. Content that only rephrases what already ranks brings nothing.

## 1. The four stages (FLOW)

- **Find**: who searches, with which words and which intent (learn, compare, buy, go somewhere); what customers ask by phone and at the counter; the gaps on the site.
- **Leverage**: what already gives the business authority (press, partners, reviews, local life) and how to get more mentions.
- **Optimise**: improve existing pages before writing new ones: titles and descriptions that make people click, answers people can quote, questions people also ask, structured data.
- **Win**: pages close to the decision (prices, comparisons, booking, quote) and the conversion: is the next step obvious, is the proof there?

Every working document follows the same shape: the visitor's intent · the evidence available now · the gaps that block trust or conversion · changes in priority order · what to measure and when to review · claims to verify.

## 2. Topic clusters

1. List candidate searches from customer words, search_web suggestions and "people also ask".
2. For pairs of searches, look at the first ten results of each (search_web or the browser) and count the addresses they share: **7-10 shared** → one page for both; **4-6** → same cluster; **2-3** → separate pages linked to each other; **0-1** → unrelated.
3. Build **one main page** per cluster (the broad subject) and **satellite pages** (precise questions), each linking to the main page and to its neighbours with clear words.
4. Show the map as a simple table or a private page: cluster, main page, satellites, intent, existing or to write.

## 3. The brief for one page

- The target search and its intent; what the first results have in common (type of page, length, format) and what they miss.
- **Information gain** (required): exactly what this page will add that no ranking page has: the owner's own data, a real case with its result, an expert's first-hand advice, an original method. "More detail" or "better layout" is not a gain.
- Outline with headings that answer real questions; where the main words go (title, H1, first paragraph, a few headings), naturally.
- Title (about 50-60 characters) and description (about 150) proposals.
- Internal links to and from existing pages; external sources to cite.
- Who signs it and why they are credible.
Sites and domains not to send traffic to (direct competitors, low-quality sites) stay out of the links.

## 4. Writing quality

- **Who**: a real author with a short bio; **how**: say when something was tested, measured, photographed by the business; **why**: to help the reader decide or do something.
- Answer first, then detail. Short paragraphs, useful subheadings, a table when comparing, real photos with text alternatives.
- Blocks people (and AI answers) can quote: a clear question as a heading and a self-contained answer of a few sentences under it.
- Final clean-up: apply "Write like a human" and remove invisible characters (zero-width spaces, odd quotation marks pasted from tools).

## 5. A plan over months

For a site that wants regular content: a 12-month plan in four phases (foundation: fix and optimise existing pages; expansion: the main clusters; scale: satellites and local pages; authority: mentions and partnerships), an editorial calendar (one realistic rhythm the owner can hold), and the indicators to watch (clicks and impressions in Search Console if connected, calls, bookings). Offer a monthly routine for the calendar.
