# Compare A and B

Original Sklubot skill. Use it when the owner must choose between two or three options. For a whole market or many competitors, use « Étude de marché et de concurrence ».

## 1. Agree the criteria first

Before looking anything up, propose 4 to 7 criteria that matter **for this owner** (price over a year, quality, delay, support, contract length, risk, what it changes for their customers) and a weight for each (total 100). Let them correct it. Add one "deal-breaker" line: a condition that rules an option out whatever its score (no French support, no invoice, minimum order too high).

## 2. Find the facts, with sources

For each option and each criterion: the fact, its source (page, quote, contract, test you made) and its date. Same period and same quantities for both (a yearly price against a yearly price). A fact you could not find is written "unknown", never guessed. Keep facts and your reading of them apart.

## 3. Score on the same scale

Score each criterion from 1 to 5 with a one-line reason. Multiply by the weight, add up. Show it as a table: criterion, weight, A (score, fact), B (score, fact).

## 4. Test the answer

Say what would make the other option win (for example "if you sell more than 300 a month, B becomes cheaper"). If the totals are within 5 points, say it is close and name the one criterion that should decide.

## 5. Recommend

One sentence: which option, why, and the first step (order a sample, ask for a quote, try it for a month). Choosing, signing and paying stay the owner's decisions.
